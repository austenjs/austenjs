# Hi, I'm Austen

**Machine learning researcher and engineer working across quantitative finance, deep learning, and formal reasoning.**

I turn research ideas into reproducible experiments and practical systems. My recent work spans neural models for market prediction, agentic financial analysis, and formal theorem proving with Lean.

## Selected work

### [DeepLOB Transformer](https://github.com/austenjs/deeplob-transformer)

An experimental framework for short-horizon price-movement prediction on the FI-2010 limit-order-book benchmark.

- Reproduces the original CNN–Inception–LSTM DeepLOB architecture
- Evaluates a Transformer encoder as the temporal backbone
- Studies order-book imbalance features and architecture ablations
- Connects classification results to a confidence-filtered trading simulation

### [Agentic Financial Agent](https://github.com/austenjs/AgenticFinancialAgent)

A Streamlit-based financial research agent developed for the Data Science Indonesia 2025 competition.

### [News Sentiment Trading Strategy](https://github.com/austenjs/news-sentiment-trading-strategy)

An end-to-end research workflow covering financial-news preprocessing, exploratory analysis, forecasting, and strategy evaluation.

### [Mathesis](https://github.com/Huawei-AI4Math/Mathesis)

Research on translating natural-language mathematics into Lean 4 statements for downstream formal theorem proving. Published at ICLR 2026.
